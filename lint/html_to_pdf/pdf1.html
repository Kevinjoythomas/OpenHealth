Engineer's Notes

1. All load calculations performed using NEC 220.82 Optional Method. General lighting, small appliance, and laundry loads subject to demand factors. All units below 10,000 VA subtotal therefore 100% demand factor applies.

2. Fixed appliances (water heater) added at 100% nameplate per NEC 220.82(B)(3). All units have gas range/oven and gas washer/dryer — no electric cooking or laundry loads included.

3. A/C load excluded from demand factor and added separately at 125% of MCA per NEC 220.82(C) and NEC 440.32. ADU units use Midea ducted inverter heat pump (condenser: NOD30-36HFN10-M2 / air handler: NT1-36HWFN10-M2-D). Indoor air handler is low voltage control only — no electrical load.

4. Minimum 100A service assigned per NEC 230.79(C) regardless of calculated demand.

5. Individual 100A main breaker panels to be installed in each unit per NEC 230.71. Four revenue meters in common metering center — coordinate with SCE prior to rough-in.

6. All work to be performed by licensed California C-10 Electrical Contractor and inspected by City of Glendale Building & Safety Division prior to energization.
